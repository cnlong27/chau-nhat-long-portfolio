About me

I graduated from Danang University of Technology in June 2017 with a major in Software Engineering. My career started at Asian Tech, where I learned game programming and Unity while working on small projects and outsourced projects.

I then joined a startup focused on puzzle games, where I grew from developer into a role that included team management, feature development, visual effects, data analysis and game marketing. Since 2020, I have worked at Gear Games across in-house and outsourced projects, contributing as a developer and senior developer.

My strongest motivation is continuous learning: exploring new technology, improving game systems and working effectively with a team.
